What is the difference between a globular cluster and a dwarf galaxy?

A dwarf galaxy:
- "the smallest gravitationally bound systems in which galaxy formation can occur"
- shallow gravitational wells, high dark matter fractions, relatively simple baryonic structure
https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1674-4527/ae715f/pdf

A globular cluster:
- a type of star cluster, characterised by its lack of dark matter and low mass-to-light ratios
https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025MNRAS.539.2485D/abstract

The differences:
- Dwarf galaxies are categorised by their dark matter levels, which are at a similar ratio regular galaxies (source?)
- Globular clusters have a lack of dark matter comparitavely, resulting in lower mass-to-light ratios
